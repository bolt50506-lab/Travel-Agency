# Environment Setup\n\nSelf-hosted mode uses local PostgreSQL and PostgREST.\n\nCopy .env.example to .env.local and set:\n- POSTGRES_DB\n- POSTGRES_USER\n- POSTGRES_PASSWORD\n- POSTGREST_DB_PASSWORD\n- POSTGREST_JWT_SECRET\n- POSTGREST_URL\n- LOCAL_AUTH_SECRET\n- NEXT_PUBLIC_APP_URL\n\nSupplier integrations remain optional:\n- FLIGHT_PROVIDER=mock or a configured production adapter\n- HOTEL_PROVIDER=mock or a configured production adapter\n- DUFFEL_API_KEY\n- Amadeus credentials\n- Travelport credentials\n- Hotelbeds credentials\n- Expedia Rapid credentials\n\nPakistani manual payments are supported for bank transfer, Raast, JazzCash and Easypaisa.\n\nNever commit .env.local, supplier credentials or payment secrets. PostgreSQL and PostgREST should remain bound to localhost.\n
